About This Page

Chord progression fun for singing circle!

How to use:

  • Transpose: Use the + and − buttons to change the key
  • View lyrics: Click any song title to expand and see the lyrics
  • Reorder songs: Drag the ⋮⋮ handle to rearrange songs in your preferred order
  • Lock songs open: Long-press (hold) a song title for 0.5 seconds to lock it with a 📌 pin
  • Switch progressions: Click the 🎼 Progression button or the gear icon near the chords
  • Customize: Click the ⚙️ Settings button to change color theme, font size, and more
  • Share: Your settings are saved in the URL - copy and share your custom arrangement!
  • Mobile app: Tap "Add to Home Screen" to save as "Medlies" with a 🎸 icon
