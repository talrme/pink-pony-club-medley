About This Page

Chord progression fun for singing circle!

How to use:

  • Use the + and − buttons to transpose chords to your preferred key
  • Drag songs to reorder them
  • Click the gear icon to switch between different progressions
  • Settings are saved in the URL so you can share your custom arrangement and save an icon to your phone's home screen
